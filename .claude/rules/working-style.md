# Working style

- **Be concise.** Report what changed, what you checked and what to decide; do not retell the work. Say plainly when something failed, was skipped or could not be verified. Offer the next step and wait for the answer.
- Use they/them for people whose pronouns are not stated.
- Ask before anything hard to undo. For decisions that are theirs (a default, a key binding, a naming scheme), pick the obvious one, say which you picked and why, and let them overrule.
- Match the surrounding code: comment density, naming, idiom. Comments say why, not what.
