# Roadmap rules

- Milestones are `M-<NAME>`, epics are three letters (`T`, `W`, `P` first for TUI, web, PWA), stories are `KEY-n`. A new story takes the next free number in its epic; numbers are never reused or renumbered, even when a story is dropped or folded into another (`REL-2` and `REL-3` into `REL-1`).
- A story is one feature. Do one at a time, finish it (tests, docs, tick), then propose the next.
- Decisions already taken, not to be reopened without being asked: recurring transactions always ask before posting; budget rollover belongs to the budget epics; the TUI and the server are for macOS and Linux only (Windows computers use the web app in a browser); languages are English, Spanish and German on every surface except `thaler cli`, which is English only; releases leave on a train every second Monday (cut-off the Friday before, see `REL-1`), versioned with SemVer (`1.0.0` ends `M-TUI`); the app-gateway is a module of the single `thaler` binary; the web app uses next-intl (react-i18next stays possible).
